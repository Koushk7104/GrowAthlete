The problem

India has talent everywhere. No system to find it.

Results sit in school certificates, WhatsApp photos and local news clippings. Scouts can't see them. Sponsors can't verify them. Athletes can't prove them.

  1. No single identity for athletes

    Achievements are scattered across certificates, photos, clippings and coach references. There is no unified, trusted record.

    GrowAthlete: one verified sports resume.

  2. Talent is invisible beyond the metros

    Athletes outside big cities lack exposure, network and personal branding. Selection still depends on who you know.

    GrowAthlete: equal exposure for rural and urban athletes.

  3. No career roadmap

    Most athletes don't know which tournaments matter, how to approach sponsors, or how to apply for trials, scholarships and academies.

    GrowAthlete: opportunities and guidance in one place.

  4. Scouting is manual and expensive

    Brands, clubs and academies struggle to find sport-specific athletes, verify performance data and track consistency.

    GrowAthlete: structured, searchable, verified data.

  5. Athletes are treated as one-time winners

    There is nowhere to track progress over years, injuries and recovery, or performance trends. One bad season and they are forgotten.

    GrowAthlete: the whole career, not one result.
