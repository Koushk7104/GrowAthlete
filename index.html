In practice

What it looks like for each side

Illustrative scenarios, not customer stories.

Athlete

A kabaddi raider in a small town

He plays well at district level but nobody outside the district has seen him. He builds a profile, gets his results verified, and shows up when a club in another state searches for raiders under 20.

Scout

A scout covering three states

Instead of travelling to every trial, she filters by sport, age and improvement, opens verified profiles, and shortlists a handful worth visiting in person.

Sponsor

A brand looking for rising talent

The team wants emerging athletes in a specific sport and region. It reviews verified results and progress over seasons before reaching out.

Academy

An academy planning its next intake

The coaches compare candidates on the same structured metrics, follow their progress between trials, and invite the ones who keep improving.

Coach

A school coach with a standout student

He confirms her achievements on her profile, so the results he has watched for years finally count outside the school gates.

Organisation

A sports body scanning a region

It uses one central database to see who is competing and improving across districts, rather than relying on paper entries.
